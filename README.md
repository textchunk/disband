# Disband

Disband takes songs apart, on your own PC.

Drop in a track and you get the vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano and everything else as separate files. It can also pull the lead vocal away from the backing vocals, break a drum stem into kick, snare, toms, hats and cymbals, take the crowd off a live recording, widen old mono recordings to stereo, bring back some of the top end that MP3s lose, and write a song out as MIDI.

It converts too: MP3, WAV, FLAC, AIFF, M4A and OGG. It goes as low as 12-bit and 26.04 kHz if you want your samples to sound like they came off an SP-1200.

Under the hood are the best-scoring open separation models available. Nothing gets uploaded anywhere.

## Download

[Get the latest version](https://github.com/textchunk/disband/releases/latest) and run `Disband-Setup-<version>.exe`. The installer isn't signed yet, so Windows may warn you. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". After that, Disband keeps itself up to date.

Disband used to be called Stems. If you have Stems installed, it turns into Disband on its own next update and keeps your models and settings.

This repository only holds the installers.
